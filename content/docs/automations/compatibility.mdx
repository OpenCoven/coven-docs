## Reading this ledger

- "Partial" means landed behavior with public evidence — it is still not a
  certification claim.
- "Target not met" means the normative behavior is ratified but not
  implemented; do not build against it as if it exists.
- The overall program status is **foundation-ready, not v1-certified**
  ([#854](https://github.com/OpenCoven/coven/issues/854)). Unattended external
  side effects remain out of scope until the authority and certification gates
  pass.

This page is part of the documentation's verification surface: when upstream
artifacts move, the [source-drift check](https://github.com/OpenCoven/coven-docs/blob/main/docs/freshness.md)
creates the review obligation that keeps these rows truthful.
